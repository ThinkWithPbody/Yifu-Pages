> [!green]- Lecture
> ZCB - Zero Carbon Balance
> 
> - 60-80% of energy use is still fossil fuel - natural gas is a marketing term, it’s methane
> - If everyone uses electricity from the grid, the peak would be 2:1 to production right now
> 	- meeting peak demand is the most expensive part because it determines max output
> 	- Buildings are good at reducing the peak and spreading out the peak electricity demand(thermal mass)
> 
> ZCB Calculation
> 
> - Net emission = Embodied Carbon + Operational Carbon - Avoided Emissions
> 	- Embodied Carbon
> 		- Upfront Carbon
> 		- Use Stage Embodied Carbon
> 		- End of Life Carbon
> 		- Design Service Life - Exactly how many years should this building last?
> 			- 80+ Years?
> 	- Operational Carbon
> 		- Direct Emissions
> 		- Indirect Emissions
> 	- Avoided Emissions
> 		- Exported Green Power
> 		- Carbon Offsets
> 		- Renewable energy potential>
> 		- Amount of carbon offsets required?
> 	- Missing Pieces
> 		- Relocate Site to reduce Transportation
> 		- Lifestyle
> - Steps to Set Climate related Goals at Project Conception
> 	1. Determine the Occupancy, GFA, and Site Area and Location
> 	2. Determine the Design Service Life
> 	3. Determine the Total Energy Use Intensity target for your building type
> 		- 80% is space and water heating
> 			- 31 Windows, 24 Air Leakage, 20 Ventilation, 16 Walls, 5 Roof, 4 Doors
> 		- Appliances, Lighting, Cooling
> 		- AIA Zero Tool
> 			- Put in building location, types, and it provides a baseline and Architecture 2030 challenge target
> 		- TGS Toronto Green Standard
> 			- Tier 3 Residential > 6 Stories => TRUI 75, TEDI 15
> 		- Electricity Maps
> 			- Electricity Grid Carbon Emissions
> 			- Split sources used (40% Electricity, 60%Gas?)
> 	4. Determine the Embodied Emissions Target
> 		- Choose 600-250 $kgCO_2e/m^2$ | 75-5 $ekWh/m^2/yr$ as starting point and move downward
> 		- Very little data in the EPD
> 		- Trouble understanding what the end of life is
> 		- Most studies only examine “core and shell”
> 		- One Whole Building LCA Accessment
> 			- LMN Architects
> 		- Climate Emergency Design Guide
> 		- Carbon Leadership Forum CLF
> 	5. Estimate the renewable energy potential
> 		- PVWatts Calculator
> 	6. Estimate  the Whole Lifecycle Carbon emissions profile
> 		- ARC2048 Whole Life Cycle Carbon Emissions Estimate Spreadsheet
> 		- Use at all stages and save iterations of this file for record keeping
> 	7. Develop preliminary Zero Carbon Balance strategies
> 		- On-site renewable energy generation?
> 		- Arrange for carbon emissions offsets?
> 
> Communicating Low Carbon Concepts
> - Our work in illustrating these concepts will help others understand the impact
> - Objectives for representation
> 	- To better understand what you design
> 	- To describe interconnected ideas/systems To describe design intent to others
> - What types of systems?
> 	- Primary mechanical systems
> 		- ventilation, heating, cooling
> 		- lighting, electrical, fire protection, conveyance / circulation
> 		- Plumbing, stormwater
> 	- Relationships between passive and active strategies
> 		- Roof and site design with rainwater collection
> 		- Daylight and passive solar collection
> 		- Renewable energy systems
> 	- Interactions between two
> 		- Automated facade, passive-active shading, seasonal modes of operation?
> - Representation of design intent
> - Diagrams as part of the design process
> - Recommendations
> 	1. Develop diagrams early in your design process to help resolve your passive/active approcach
> 	2. Draw more than the mechanical systems - consider relationships from climate to site to space
> 	3. Determine what additional information / analysis is needed to convey the intent
> 	4. Augment your drawings with precise information where possible
> 		- No reason why you shouldn’t use lots of words and numbers to describe and explain the space - what does it feel life, sound like, smell like?
> 	5. Consider how to illustrate different modes of operation - dynamic processes
